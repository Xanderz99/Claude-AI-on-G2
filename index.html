Talk from the glasses, like Even AI

Press and hold the temple and ask your question, then let go. You can also double-tap to start and tap (or just pause) to finish. The glasses show a live sound meter while listening and then "Thinking...". Your iPhone runs the Shortcut , which transcribes your voice, asks Claude, and sends the answer back to the glasses.

With a Groq key in Settings, your phone transcribes the question itself, so use this shorter Shortcut:

  1. In Shortcuts, make a new shortcut named exactly .
  2. Add the Claude app's Ask Claude action with Shortcut Input as the prompt. You can add "Answer briefly in plain text."
  3. Add Get Contents of URL: the address above, Method POST, Request Body File → Response.
  4. Optional: add Open App → Even at the end so you land back in the Even app.

iOS only lets an app open a Shortcut while the app is on screen. Your iPhone has to be unlocked with the Even app open. If the Shortcut doesn't start, tap Run Shortcut above. Without a Groq key, recordings are uploaded to your private topic on ntfy and deleted automatically after a few hours.

Ask with Siri (iPhone mic)
  1. Make a shortcut named Ask Claude on G2.
  2. Add Dictate Text (or Ask for Input if you'd rather type).
  3. Optional: add Get Contents of URL: the address above, Method POST, header Title = question, Request Body File → Dictated Text.
  4. Add Ask Claude with Dictated Text as the prompt.
  5. Add Get Contents of URL: the address above, Method POST, Request Body File → Response.
  6. Say "Hey Siri, Ask Claude on G2". Try it with your iPhone locked: it may run without unlocking, depending on the Claude app.

Groq (free, optional)

A free key from console.groq.com makes this app faster and more reliable. Your question is transcribed on the phone with Groq's Whisper, and appears on the glasses as soon as you let go. Recordings are no longer uploaded to ntfy, and Apple's Transcribe Audio isn't needed.

You can also set Answers from to Groq. Answers then come straight from Groq's free open models (not Claude) and appear word by word. No Shortcut is involved, so this works with your iPhone locked in your pocket, like Even AI. The free tier has daily limits, and Groq may use a model it later retires; pick another in Settings if that happens.
